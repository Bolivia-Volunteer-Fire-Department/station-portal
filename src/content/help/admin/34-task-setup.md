# Tasks & Checklists Setup

*Reports & Logs → Task Setup* configures recurring task routines, station chores, and vehicle inspection checklists.

IMAGE HERE

## Creating Task and Checklist Templates

Click **Add Template** to create a new task or apparatus inspection routine:

- **Template Name & Description** — A clear title (such as *Morning Station Routine* or *Engine 1 Daily Apparatus Inspection*) and operational instructions for members.
- **Apparatus Link** — Associate the checklist with a specific vehicle from the department fleet, or leave unassigned for general facility tasks.
- **Effective Dates** — Set when the template becomes active and optional sunset dates when an updated checklist revision takes effect.

## Scheduling & Recurrence

The **Scheduling** card determines when the task appears on member dashboards and in the Today's Tasks widget:

- **Daily** — Generated every calendar day for duty crews.
- **Specific Days of the Week** — Generated on selected days (for example, *Monday, Wednesday, Friday* chore rotations).
- **Weekly or Monthly** — Scheduled on fixed intervals (such as weekly deep cleaning or monthly generator tests).
- **On Demand / Manual** — Kept in the library for members to log whenever required without a daily prompt.

## Checklist Builder & Permissions

Configure checklist items and access rules:

- **Form Builder Canvas** — Add check items, pass/fail inspections, numeric gauge inputs (tire PSI, fuel levels), and note fields.
- **Access Controls** — Specify which member roles can view the checklist, start drafts, electronically sign and stamp completions, or unlock signed entries for corrections.
