# Asset Configuration & Setup

*Administration → Assets → Asset Setup* lets administrators customize the equipment and apparatus types used across your station.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Configuring asset categories and types requires the **Set up equipment & apparatus** permission.

## Why configure asset types?

Every fire department operates unique response vehicles and issues specialized personal gear. Rather than being locked into rigid options, Asset Setup lets you define exactly what your department uses in the field.

When you add or edit types here, they immediately become available in the dropdown menus on the Equipment and Apparatus tabs.

## Available asset categories

Asset Setup organizes equipment into three primary groups:

- **Apparatus Types**: Vehicle categories such as Engines, Aerial Ladders, Heavy Rescues, Brush Trucks, Water Tenders, Ambulances, and Squads.
- **Turnout Gear Types**: Personal protective equipment (PPE) items such as Bunker Coats, Bunker Pants, Structural Helmets, Fire Boots, Gloves, and Nomex Hoods.
- **Radio Types**: Field communication devices such as Portable Radios, Mobile Vehicle Rigs, Station Base Stations, and Minitor Pagers.

## Adding or editing an asset type

To create a new asset type or adjust an existing one:

1. Click **Add Type** in the top right corner, or click the **Pencil icon** next to an existing type.
2. Select the **Asset Category** (Apparatus, Turnout Gear, or Radios).
3. Enter a friendly **Type Name** (for example, "Wildland Coat" or "Tactical Rescue Rig").
4. Enter an optional **Code Identifier** (a simple lowercase key such as `wildland_coat`). If left blank, the system creates one automatically.
5. Set an optional **Sort Order** number. Lower numbers appear first in selection menus throughout the app.
6. Click **Save changes** to apply your updates.

> [!NOTE]
> System defaults are provided out of the box. You can customize any default type at any time or add new options that match your department standard operating procedures.
