# Shift Notes Setup

*Reports & Logs → Note Setup* configures templates and categories for station shift handover logs, officer pass-downs, and incident notes.

IMAGE HERE

## Configuring Note Templates

Shift note templates give duty crews structured formats for passing important operational information between shifts:

- **Template Name** — Titles such as *Shift Handover Log*, *Station Officer Pass-down*, or *Facility Notice*.
- **Description & Instructions** — Guidance on what information offgoing and oncoming crews should document.
- **Structured Fields** — Configure prompts for apparatus status, pending tasks, facility issues, and key incidents handled during the shift.

## Note Categories & Tags

Organize note entries by department function:

- **Operations & Incidents** — Significant emergency calls, response notes, and mutual-aid dispatches.
- **Apparatus & Facilities** — Maintenance notifications, supply deliveries, and equipment out-of-service notices.
- **Administrative & Memos** — Directives from department leadership and duty officer reminders.

## Role Permissions for Shift Notes

Control who participates in the note log:

- **View Notes** — Allow members to review historical notes and stay informed on station activities.
- **Create & Edit Notes** — Grant permission to duty officers, shift leaders, or all members to log handover reports.
- **Electronic Signatures & Locking** — Require author signatures to lock reports and preserve an unalterable log for station records.
