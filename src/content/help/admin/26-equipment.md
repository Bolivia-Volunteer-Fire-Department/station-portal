# Equipment Tracking

*Assets → Equipment* lets you track fire department gear, radios, and SCBA breathing air cylinders. It also watches required safety inspection cycles and retirement dates so your station stays compliant with NFPA standards and state laws.

## Equipment categories

IMAGE HERE

The equipment manager is organized into three main categories:

| Category | What it tracks | Key timelines |
|---|---|---|
| **Turnout Gear** | Bunker coats, bunker pants, boots, gloves, Nomex hoods, and helmets | 10-year NFPA 1851 retirement date and 12-month inspection cycles |
| **Radios** | Portable radios, mobile radios, base stations, and pagers | Serial numbers, model names, and crew assignments |
| **SCBA Cylinders** | Composite and aluminum self-contained breathing air bottles | 5-year hydrostatic test interval and 15-year maximum service life |

## Turnout gear lifecycles

Structural firefighting gear protects firefighters from extreme heat, but protective fibers break down over time. Under NFPA 1851 standards, turnout gear must be taken out of service when it reaches its expiration date or misses safety checks.

- **10-Year NFPA Retirement**: Every bunker coat, pant, helmet, boot, glove, and Nomex hood must be retired no later than 10 years after its manufacture date. The system automatically calculates this date and shows countdown badges as gear approaches its retirement limit.
- **12-Month Inspection Cycle**: Turnout gear must undergo routine safety inspections at least once every 12 months. The system warns officers when an inspection is due within 60 days or overdue.
- **Assigning to Members**: You can assign any piece of turnout gear directly to an active member. Assigned gear appears on the member's personal profile card.

## Tracking radios

Radios are critical for firefighter communication and safety on emergency scenes.

- **Serial Number Tracking**: Record the manufacturer serial number, station radio ID (such as Portable 12), and make or model.
- **Member Assignment**: Assign portable radios to specific members or keep them in station pool inventory. When assigned to a member, the radio serial number and ID appear in the member's profile.

## SCBA composite cylinder testing

Breathing air cylinders hold air under very high pressure and require strict testing to prevent dangerous failures.

- **5-Year Hydrostatic Test**: Composite bottles must be pressure-tested (hydro tested) every 5 years to verify cylinder strength. The system calculates the next hydro test deadline from the last test date.
- **15-Year Service Life Limit**: Most composite SCBA bottles have a strict 15-year maximum lifespan from the original manufacture date, after which they must be permanently retired.

## Status badges and alerts

Equipment rows display color-coded status badges so officers can spot problems quickly:

- **Good / In Service (Green)**: The asset has passed all inspections and has plenty of service life remaining.
- **Due Soon (Amber)**: An annual inspection or 5-year hydro test is due within the next 60 days, or a piece of gear is within 180 days of its 10-year retirement.
- **Overdue / Expired (Red)**: The item has passed its inspection deadline, exceeded its 5-year hydro interval, or passed its mandatory retirement date. Expired equipment should be removed from front-line service immediately.

> [!NOTE]
> Members can check their assigned turnout gear and radios anytime by going to **User Profile** from the main menu.
