# Work Orders Management

*Assets → Work Orders* enables officers and administrators to track station repair requests, apparatus maintenance tickets, equipment defects, and facility work orders.

IMAGE HERE

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Viewing and managing work orders requires the **Manage Work Orders** or **View Work Orders** permission.

## Work Orders Overview

IMAGE HERE

The Work Orders module centralizes issue tracking across the department:

- **Apparatus Defect Reports**: Issues reported during daily apparatus inspections or post-incident maintenance checks.
- **Equipment Repairs**: SCBA, turnout gear, hand tools, chainsaws, and portable radio repair requests.
- **Station & Facilities**: Bay door issues, generator servicing, HVAC repairs, plumbing, and station maintenance.

## Managing Tickets

Administrators can review incoming tickets, assign technicians, update statuses, and log repair costs:

1. Filter work orders by status (**Open**, **In Progress**, **Resolved**, or **Closed**).
2. Click any work order to view details, submission history, priority level, and attached repair photos or documentation.
3. Update ticket assignments to internal maintenance officers or external vendors.
4. Record completion notes and resolution dates when repairs are finalized.
