# User Profile & Settings

Your **User Profile** is where you manage your account details, check your certifications, and customize how StationSync works for you.

To open your User Profile, simply click on your name at the very top of the sidebar. The page is split into two tabs: **My Profile** and **My Settings**.

## The My Profile Tab

The **My Profile** tab brings together your personal details, your credentials, and your account security in one place.

### Personal Information

The **Personal Information** card lets you view your department identity and update your contact info:

- **Name, Rank, and Role** — These display your official department title and permissions. They are managed by station officers, so you cannot edit them here.
- **Email Address & Phone Number** — Enter your current contact information so the department can reach you.
- **FEMA Student ID (SID)** — Enter the Student Identification number from your FEMA training transcripts so it is on file for department reporting.

Click **Save Personal Info** after making any changes. Only you and station administrators can see your contact details.

### Profile Picture

The **Profile Picture** card lets you choose how you appear across the app:

- You can upload a photo from your computer or phone, or take a new picture using your camera.
- Your photo appears next to your name and in department lists so crew members can easily recognize you.

### Certifications

The **Certifications** section is an expandable card on your profile (collapsed by default to save space):

- Click anywhere on the card header to expand it and view your tracked certifications (such as EMT, CPR, or Driver/Operator).
- Each certification shows its current status (**Current**, **Expires soon**, or **Expired**) and its expiration date.
- If an officer attached a scan or PDF of your certificate, click **View upload** to inspect the file.
- When a certification is nearing its expiration date, the app will also remind you at sign-in so you have plenty of time to renew it!

### Change Password

The **Change Password** card allows you to update your login password at any time:

1. Type your new password into both boxes to confirm it.
2. Click **Update Password**.
3. Changing your password signs out any other devices you left logged in, keeping your account safe and secure.

## The My Settings Tab

The **My Settings** tab lets you customize the appearance, sounds, and alerts for this device.

### Preferences

The **Preferences** card contains simple switches to tailor the app to your liking:

- **Theme** — Choose between Light Mode and Dark Mode, or follow your device settings.
- **Sound Effects** — Turn interface clicks and alert chimes on or off.
- **Time Format** — Choose between 12-hour time (like 2:00 PM) and 24-hour military time (like 14:00). All calendars and clock history will use your choice.
- **Hide Events by Default** — If you only want to see shifts on your calendars, you can turn this on to hide meetings and trainings by default.

Click **Save Preferences** to save your selections.

### Push Notifications

The **Notifications** card lets you receive instant alerts on your phone, tablet, or computer:

- **Enable Notifications** — Click this button to register your current device. When your browser asks for permission, click **Allow**.
- **Shift Alerts** — Get notified immediately when an officer approves or declines your offer to fill an open shift.
- **Announcements** — Get notified when an officer posts an urgent station announcement.
- **Turn Off** — If you want to stop receiving alerts on this device, click **Turn off**.

Each device is registered separately. Setting up notifications on your phone will not turn them on for your desktop computer, so be sure to enable notifications on each device you use.

### Your Access

The **Your Access** card shows a complete list of what your assigned role allows you to do:

- It lists all permissions granted to your account, such as using the timeclock, viewing schedules, or signing trainings.
- If you ever wonder why a button is missing or why you cannot see a certain screen, check this list. If you need access to something for your duties, an administrator can update your role.
