# Roster

The **Roster** module is the department directory of all active personnel, showing member names, ranks, and active qualifications.

IMAGE HERE

## Viewing the Department Roster

The roster provides a quick reference to see who serves in your department:

- **Member Name** — Personnel listed in alphabetical order.
- **Rank & Insignia** — Official department rank, displayed with the department's custom rank color and badge icon.
- **Active Qualifications & Certifications** — Columns showing which members currently hold active credentials (such as EMT, Driver/Operator, Hazmat, or Officer certifications).

IMAGE HERE

## Searching the Roster

Use the search bar at the top of the table to quickly find a member:

1. Type a first or last name into the **Search by name…** box.
2. Click **Search** (or press Enter).
3. The table filters immediately to matching personnel.
4. Click **Clear** to return to the full roster.
