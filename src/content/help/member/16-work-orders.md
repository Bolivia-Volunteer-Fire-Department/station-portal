# Work Orders

The **Work Orders** module allows members to report, track, and follow up on equipment malfunctions, vehicle repair needs, and station maintenance issues.

## Submitting a Work Order

If you spot something broken or in need of maintenance:

1. Click **Submit Work Order** (or **New Request**).
2. Choose what is affected: **Apparatus**, **Equipment**, or **Station / Facility**.
3. Select the specific item (such as *Engine 1* or *Thermal Imaging Camera*).
4. Provide a clear title and description of the defect or issue.
5. Choose the **Priority Level**:
   - **Low** — Minor cosmetic or non-urgent repair.
   - **Medium** — Functional issue that does not prevent emergency response.
   - **High** — Important operational issue requiring prompt officer attention.
   - **Critical / Out of Service** — Severe mechanical or safety defect that takes the vehicle or gear out of service immediately.
6. Attach helpful photos or documents (such as damage pictures or leak locations) if needed.
7. Click **Submit**. Station officers and maintenance personnel are alerted to the issue.

## Tracking Ticket Status

The work orders table keeps everyone informed on repair progress:

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Reported** | The issue has been logged and is awaiting maintenance review |
| **In Progress** | A technician, vendor, or officer is actively repairing the item |
| **Waiting on Parts** | Work is paused while replacement parts are on order |
| **Resolved** | Repairs are finished and the equipment is back in full service |

## Adding Notes and Updates

You can open any active work order to read repair progress notes from officers, post additional information, or upload more photos as work proceeds.
