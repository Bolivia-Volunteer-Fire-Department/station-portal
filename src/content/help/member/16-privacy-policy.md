# Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

StationSync is dedicated to protecting the privacy of emergency service organizations and their members. This Privacy Policy outlines what information we collect, how it is used, and how it is secured.

### 1. Information We Collect

To provide our scheduling, operational management, and attendance features, StationSync collects:

* **Account Information:** Name, email address, assigned department rank, identification numbers, and contact details.
* **Operational Records:** Shift attendance, training records, certifications, checklist entries, apparatus work orders, and internal station announcements.
* **Location Data:** When clocking in or out via mobile or web devices, geographic coordinates may be evaluated to verify on-premises station presence in accordance with your department's attendance rules. Location is only evaluated during active timeclock actions and is not tracked continuously in the background.
* **Device and Notification Identifiers:** Push notification device tokens and browser platform identifiers to route shift notifications and system alerts to your devices.
* **Communications:** Messages sent within StationSync station chat channels and direct officer dispatches.

### 2. How Information is Used

We use the collected information exclusively to:

* Facilitate station duty tracking, timeclock calculations, and scheduling rosters.
* Notify members of upcoming shifts, shift offer approvals, training requirements, and station announcements.
* Support departmental compliance, certification auditing, and administrative oversight.
* Maintain application security, verify authentication, and troubleshoot technical issues.

### 3. Data Ownership and Department Control

> [!NOTE]
> All operational department records, logs, and member rosters remain the property of the subscribing emergency services department. StationSync does not sell member information or operational records to third-party advertisers.

### 4. Data Security

We implement rigorous technical safeguards, including:

* Encryption in transit (TLS/HTTPS) and encryption at rest for all operational databases and file storage.
* Granular role-based access controls and Firestore security rules restricting data access strictly to authorized personnel.
* Password hashing and enterprise authentication safeguards.

### 5. Access and Retention

Members may review and update their personal settings, contact details, and notification preferences at any time via the User Profile module. Operational logs and training records are retained in accordance with your department's record retention policies.

### 6. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, please consult your department's system administrator or contact StationSync support.
