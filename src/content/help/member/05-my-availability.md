**My Availability** is where you tell the department which shifts you *could* work. It is a list of opportunities you can claim an interest in, but it does not guarantee you would be scheduled for those.

## How the module works

Your options are the station's **availability windows** — the recurring weekly patterns an administrator sets up in *Scheduling → Availability Windows* (things like "Tuesday night", "Saturday day"). Each window has a nickname, a start and end time, and the days it runs on.

The calendar shows the windows that fall in the month you are looking at, and you mark the days you could work. **Nothing is filtered by rank**: a window is an hour of the station's week, and if you can work it, say so. An officer decides who actually gets the shift.

You will also see **events** on this calendar — training, meetings, details. They sit among the windows in time order, in their own color, and they are **not markable**: an event is not a window, so you cannot claim availability for it. Click **Hide events** above the calendar to see only the windows (it reads **Show events** while they are hidden); it resets when you reload the page.

1. Click on the windows you could work.
2. The header shows how many changes are pending, such as **3 unsaved changes**.
3. Press **Save availability** to save them.

- **Discard** throws the pending marks away and puts you back to what is stored.
- Marking a day back to what it already was removes it from the pending count, so the number never includes a change that would do nothing.
- **Past dates are shown but cannot be changed** — you cannot rewrite history.
- Switching to another month keeps your pending marks, so you can make a month's worth of choices and save once.
- If you navigate a long way back or forward, the app may say that your saved marks for that month are not loaded — press **Load *month*** and they arrive. It will not let you save a month it has not loaded, so you can never wipe marks by looking at the wrong screen.

> [!WARNING]
> Because saving is manual, closing the tab with unsaved marks loses them. The pending count in the header is the reminder to save.

## The color coding

A day you have marked is drawn filled green, a day you have not is outlined. The pill carries the window's **nickname**; hover it for the hours it covers and to see whether it is marked.

## How administrators use this

Administrators can see your chosen availability once it's saved — the Member Availability tab lists each day's windows with the names of everyone who claimed them. It is *your* responsibility to keep your availability up to date and accurate.

## If a window you expect is missing

Windows are set up once for the whole station in *Scheduling → Availability Windows*, so a missing one is a configuration question rather than a personal one. Two things worth knowing before you ask:

- A window only appears on the days it is **ticked for**, and the day it is ticked for is the day it **starts** — a Tuesday night that runs to 08:00 belongs to Tuesday, not Wednesday.
- A window with an **end date** in the past has been retired: it no longer offers anything, and it is kept only so last year's availability still reads.

If something you genuinely work is not on the list, ask an administrator to add it.
