**My Availability** is where you tell the department which shifts you *could* work. It is a list of opportunities you can claim an interest in, but it does not guarantee you would be scheduled for those.

## How the module works

The calendar preloads every shift in the month that **your rank qualifies you to fill**. That is the only list you need to look at — anything you could not take is not shown, so you never have to work out whether a shift is open to you.

You will also see **events** on this calendar — training, meetings, details. They are drawn above the shifts, in their own color, and they are **not markable**: an event is not a shift, so you cannot claim availability for it. Click **Hide events** above the calendar to see only the shifts (it reads **Show events** while they are hidden); it resets when you reload the page.

1. Click on the shifts you could work.
2. The header shows how many changes are pending, such as **3 unsaved changes**.
3. Press **Save availability** to save them.

- **Discard** throws the pending marked shifts away and puts you back to what is stored.
- Marking a shift back to what it already was removes it from the pending count, so the number never includes a change that would do nothing.
- **Past dates are shown but cannot be changed** — you cannot rewrite history.
- Switching to another month keeps your pending marks, so you can make a month's worth of choices and save once.

> [!WARNING]
> Because saving is manual, closing the tab with unsaved marks loses them. The pending count in the header is the reminder to save.

## The color coding

A marked shift is drawn filled, an unmarked one is outlined, and the color of the pill comes from the shift's assignment — the same colors used on My Schedule. A shift's nickname, if it has one, replaces the times; hover for the exact window.

## How administrators use this

Administrators can see your chosen availability once it's saved. However, it is *your* responsibility to keep your availability up to date and accurate.

## If a shift you want is missing

Your **rank** determines what shifts are available to you. The other possibility is that the shift exists on the schedule but not as a weekly template — one-off custom shifts are not part of the recurring pattern, so they do not appear here. If a shift that should be there is not, ask an administrator to check your rank.
