## Customizing your dashboard

Each member can personalize their dashboard layout to fit their daily routine:

1. Click the **Customize** button in the top right corner of the Dashboard.
2. Use the **Left** and **Right** arrow controls to reposition widgets across the grid.
3. Toggle widget widths between 1 Column, 2 Columns, or Full Width.
4. Add extra cards using the **Add Widget** drawer, or remove cards you do not need.
5. Click **Save** to remember your personalized layout.
6. Click **Reset to Station Default** at any time to restore the department standard layout.

## Clocking in and out

If your role allows timeclock access, you will see options to log your shift:

1. Press **Clock In** when your shift begins, or **Clock Out** when you finish.
2. Allow the location prompt if your browser asks. Your position is recorded with your entry.
3. The card updates immediately to reflect your active duty status.

Only one action is shown at a time — **Clock In** while off duty, and **Clock Out** while on duty. If you attempt to clock in while already clocked in, the app prevents duplicate entries.

If asked to re-enter your password before clocking in, confirm your credentials and the clock action will run automatically.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Clocking in and out requires an internet connection.** A clock entry verifies the exact time and location you arrived at the station. If you have no network signal, the buttons temporarily disable and explain why; as soon as connectivity returns, they re-enable automatically.

## Location verification

Clock entries verify your location so the department can confirm station presence:

- **Location prompt declined** — If location permissions are blocked in your browser, allow location access in your device or browser settings and try again.
- **Outside station radius** — If your department enforces a geofence around the station, clocking in is accepted only within the designated distance. The message displays your current distance from the station and the maximum allowed limit.

## Currently on duty

The **Who Is On Duty** card lists all department personnel currently clocked in, along with how long they have been on shift. The list updates in real time as members arrive and leave.

## Station announcements

Recent department announcements appear in the sidebar and at the top of your dashboard so you never miss critical operational updates.

## If something looks wrong

- **Buttons are grayed out** — Check your internet connection. Once reconnected, the buttons become active again.
- **Missing clock-out** — If you forgot to clock out at the end of a shift, your entry remains open. Ask an officer or administrator to close and adjust your shift.
- **Incorrect hours** — Contact an administrator to make any needed corrections to your time record.
