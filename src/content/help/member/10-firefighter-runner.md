A small hidden game, and the one part of the app with a station-wide scoreboard.

## Finding it

Click on **something** a few times. Once the game is open, clicking the logo returns you to the dashboard. The game is an easter egg, so nothing in the app points at it — nothing that matters is affected by playing it.

## Playing

Jump the hydrants and the parked firetrucks (**Space** or **↑**) and duck the helicopters (**↓**). On a phone, the two pads under the game do the same thing — **DUCK** on the left, **JUMP** on the right. Every so often a **boss** takes over: the chief floats in on the right and throws fireballs — and has something to say between them. High ones you duck under, low ones you jump over, never the same height twice in a row, and quicker the longer it stays. When it drifts up out of the frame the ordinary run resumes, a little faster each time.

The game has music and sound effects, with **MUSIC** and **SOUNDS** buttons at the top to switch each off.

## The leaderboard

**BOARD** at the top, beside the sound buttons, opens the station's leaderboard: everyone's personal best, highest first, with your own line picked out in yellow. It shows the top 25 and says how many members have a score in total, so "top 25 of 40" means forty of you have played and there are more behind the ones on screen. Escape, the ✕ or **CLOSE** puts it away, and a run in progress pauses while the board is open — it carries on from exactly where it was when you close it.

Your score is saved when a run ends, and if it beats your own best the board opens by itself to show you where it landed. If it does not beat your best, the board stays shut and the game-over screen tells you what your best still is.

Playing does not need the board to be reachable. If a score cannot be saved, the game-over screen says so and the game carries on as normal.
