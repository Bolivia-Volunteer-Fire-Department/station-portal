## Navigating the Guides

The sidebar on the left lists all available topics. Click on any topic to read its guide. Each guide explains how that module works, what the different buttons and indicators mean, and answers common questions.

IMAGE HERE

- **Always available** — Help guides are built directly into the application, so you can read them at any time, even on a truck or tablet with no internet connection.
- **Member vs. Administration guides** — The guides listed here cover member features such as timeclocks, schedules, training records, and documents. If you have administrative permissions, you will also see dedicated guides for administration tabs when accessing the Administration panel.
- **Quick links** — Many error messages and module cards include direct links that take you straight to the relevant guide.
