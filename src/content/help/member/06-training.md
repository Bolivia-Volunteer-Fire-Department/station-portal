The **Training** module is the department's training record that replaces paper records. Each row is a training activity the department held, and you sign the ones you attended. Administrators can then see who has signed each one.

> [!NOTE]
> An administrator must activate this module for your role in order for you to use it.

## Adding or editing trainings

Depending on your role, a form appears above the list and each row gains an **Edit** action.

- The form takes a date, title, start time, duration in hours, location, instructors and a narrative, plus the classification checkboxes.
- **A date and a title are required** — without them there is nothing to display or sign, so the form will not save.
- Editing changes the training for everyone, since everyone signs the same activity.

**A training can only be edited until the first person signs it.** After that the record is evidence, not a draft, and its **Edit** action is greyed out for everyone — corrections move to the Administration module, where the change is a deliberate step. The tooltip on a greyed-out Edit says which of the two reasons applies.

You cannot **delete** a training: that would throw away other members' signatures with it. If something needs to be fixed, let an administrator know.

## Signing a training

1. Work down the list and click **Sign** on the trainings you attended.
2. The button changes to **Ready to sign** so you can see what is waiting.
3. When you have marked everything as appropriate, press **Save signatures** once.

The header keeps a count of your changes, such as **3 unsaved signatures**, so you can tell at a glance whether anything is pending.

## A signature cannot be withdrawn

Once a training shows **Signed**, the button is disabled and stays that way. That is deliberate:

- A signature is a record that you attended, and is considered the equivalent of a physical signature on a piece of paper.
- An administrator **can not** sign for you.
- If you clicked **Sign** by mistake, or the training was entered twice, **ask an administrator**. They can *remove* your signature, after which you are free to sign again.

## Filtering and your training hours

Above the list is a **Filter & sort** bar, and under it are totals for whatever the filters leave showing:

| Control | What it narrows to |
|---|---|
| **From date** / **To date** | A date range. Either end can be left open |
| **Location** | One location from those your trainings were held at |
| **My signature** | *Signed only* or *Not signed only* |
| **Sort by** | Date (newest or oldest first), title, or longest duration |

The totals are **Trainings shown**, **Training hours**, and how many of those you have signed. The hours figure is the sum of the durations in the filtered list — so setting a date range and choosing *Signed only* gives you, for example, "12.5 hrs" of training you have signed this year. The count next to the list heading follows the same filter, reading *3 of 40 signed* when a filter is on.

**Clear filters** resets everything. A training with no duration recorded simply adds nothing to the hours total; it is not skipped from the list.

## The Ext. column

The **Ext.** column shows a tick when a training has been recorded in an external system, such as a state or county reporting system. That is administrative bookkeeping you do not set or change — and once it is ticked, the training and its signatures are **completely locked**, for everyone including administrators. You will still see it in the list and can still read it; nothing about it will change again.

## If something looks wrong

- **A training you attended is not listed.** Ask an administrator to add it if you can't add it yourself.
- **You signed, but the count did not change.** The save may have failed; the message above the list says so. Nothing is recorded until you press **Save signatures**.
- **Somebody else's name is on a training you attended.** Signatures belong to the person who made them, so nobody can sign on your behalf, and an administrator cannot sign for you either.
