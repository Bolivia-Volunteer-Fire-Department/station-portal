An optional communication tool for the station.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Access to the **Chat** interface is controlled by role permissions. If you don't see the chat and you are expecting to, ask an administrator for help.

## Two ways in

- **The round button** at the bottom-right of every screen. It shows a number when there are messages you have not read, and the panel it opens floats over the page — so you can answer somebody while you are still on the schedule, the roster or anywhere else.
- **Chat** in the sidebar. The same panel, full width, for when you would rather have it in the page than over it.

Either way it is one conversation, not two: open one, close the other, and you carry on where you left off.

## The room list

Every room your role, your rank or you yourself are named by, in the order the station put them in. Each row shows the last message and, if there is anything new, how many messages have arrived since you last looked.

Open a room and it is marked read. **Mute this room** (the speaker button, once a room is open) stops a room counting towards your number while still showing it in the list — muted is *quiet*, not *hidden*.

## Sending, editing and removing

Type in the box at the bottom. **Enter** sends, **Shift+Enter** starts a new line, and the smiley button opens a small emoji picker with the ones you have used last at the top.

Hover a message — or use the keyboard, the controls appear when you tab to them — to **edit** or **remove** it:

- **Edit** changes your own text, and the message is marked *edited* afterwards, so everybody can see that it changed.
- **Remove** does not erase the message. It empties the text and leaves it in place, saying **Deleted by author**. A conversation with a hole in it reads like nonsense, so the gap stays visible — and if an officer removes somebody else's message it says **Deleted by [their name]** instead, so a message removed by an officer is on the record as that.

Which of these you can do depends on four separate role permissions: editing your own, removing your own, editing anybody's, and removing anybody's. A station can hand out one without the others.

## When something arrives

If the panel is shut, a message brings a small notice — unless the room is muted. The number on the round button is the total you have not read, across every room.

## Private conversations

**Message a member**, at the top of the room list, starts a conversation nobody else can see.

- **Only members whose role grants Chat are offered.** If somebody is missing from the list, their role cannot use chat at all — an officer can change that in Administration → Roles. Nothing is hidden from you: the list is exactly who you can talk to.
- **Pick one person, or several.** A group conversation works the same way, up to 12 members including you.
- **It has no name of its own.** It is called whatever the other members are called, from your side: to you it says "Ben", and to Ben it says you. That is also why nobody can rename it — there is nothing to rename.
- **Starting one twice does not make two.** Press "message" on Ben again, or Ben presses it on you, and you both land in the conversation you already had.
- A private conversation does **not** appear in Administration → Chat Rooms. It is yours, not the station's.

## Sounds

Chat makes six noises, and each one means something specific:

| Sound | When |
|---|---|
| **Opening a conversation** | You open a room, or move from one room to another |
| **Closing a conversation** | You go back to the room list — closing the panel with a room open does not count, because the conversation is still open behind it |
| **Sending / receiving** | You send a message, or one arrives in the room you are looking at |
| **A notification** | A message arrives in a room you are **not** looking at. This is the loudest of the six, because it is the one asking for your attention |
| **Somebody joins** | A new participant appears in the conversation (including when that is you) |
| **Somebody leaves** | A participant is no longer in the conversation (including when that is not you) |

Two rules run through all of them:

- **A muted room is silent.** Muting a room already stops it counting towards your number, and it stops its sounds too.
- **Your own message never rings at you.** It makes the send sound once, when it goes — not again when it echoes back, and not when your own badge moves.

Everything goes through the app's own sound engine, so the **sound switch in My Settings** applies to chat as well, and an administrator can change how loud each of the six is on the **Debug** page — alongside every other sound the app ships.

## Sending needs a connection

Chat is live, so messages arrive as they are sent. Sending, though, goes through the server rather than straight from your browser — that is what stops anybody writing a message that *looks* like it came from somebody else — so a message needs a connection to send.

**While you are offline the send button is greyed out and says so.** Your text stays in the box until you close the room, and it sends once the connection is back and the button is live again.

## If a room is missing

Rooms are set up by an officer in *Administration → Chat Rooms*, and who can see each one is decided there. If you expect a room and cannot see it, that is the answer to give them.
