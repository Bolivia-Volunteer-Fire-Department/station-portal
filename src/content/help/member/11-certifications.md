# My Certifications

**My Certifications** lists the certifications the station has recorded for you, with the dates they run from and to, and where each one stands today.

The page is a readout, deliberately: your certifications are recorded by an administrator, and there is nothing here to change. Ask an administrator if something is missing, wrong, or has been renewed and not yet recorded.

## The scan, where there is one

Where an officer attached the certificate itself — a scan or a photo of the card — the record carries a **View upload** button beside it, which opens the file in a new tab.

> [!NOTE]
> The link is asked for each time you press it, and the server hands one over only to you and to the officers who keep these records. Nothing is emailed to anybody, and there is nothing here for you to change: if a scan is missing, or it is the wrong one, that is an administrator's to fix.

## What the statuses mean

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Current** | You hold it today |
| **Expires soon** | It runs out within the warning period the station set for it |
| **Not yet effective** | It starts on a date in the future |
| **Expired** | The end date has passed — speak to an administrator |

## Being told before it lapses

Where the station has set a warning period for a certification, you are told **when you sign in**: a notice at the top of the Dashboard names what is running out and how long is left. It can be put away for the session, and it comes back next time you sign in until the certification stops being a problem.

> [!NOTE]
> Certifications with no warning period set are never mentioned this way. They are still listed here, with their dates and status — the station has simply decided nobody needs reminding about that one.

## Renewals

A renewal is recorded as a **new period**, so your history stays: the old dates remain on the page alongside the new ones rather than being overwritten.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Nothing expires from this page, and nothing renews itself. If your certification has been renewed in the real world, an administrator has to record it — the app only knows what it has been told.
