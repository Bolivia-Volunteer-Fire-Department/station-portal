# Tasks & Notes

The **Tasks & Notes** module is the station's central log for daily chores, apparatus checklist inspections, and shift handover notes.

IMAGE HERE

## The Three Kinds of Entries

1. **Daily Tasks** — Routine operational checklists, station cleaning assignments, and recurring maintenance chores.
2. **Apparatus Checklists** — Vehicle readiness inspections (fluids, SCBA checks, lights, tools) tied directly to a specific truck.
3. **Shift Notes** — Information passed between crews, such as equipment needing repair, open station issues, or notes from emergency calls.

IMAGE HERE

## Completing a Checklist or Task

1. Open **Tasks & Notes** from the sidebar (or click a task from the **Today's Scheduled Tasks** widget on your Dashboard).
2. Click **Start** (or click a draft in progress to resume).
3. Complete the questions, enter gauge readings, or tick off checklist items.
4. If you need to step away before finishing, click **Save Draft**.
5. When complete, click **Sign & Complete**. Your name, rank, and the current timestamp are electronically stamped on the entry to lock and finalize it.

## Logging Shift Notes

To leave a note for oncoming crews or station officers:

1. Click **New Entry** and select a shift note template.
2. Enter a title and write your notes, observations, or handover remarks.
3. Save or sign the note so it appears in the chronological station log.

## Printing & Records

Click **Print** on any completed entry or checklist to generate an official formatted paper record or PDF inspection sheet for department records.
